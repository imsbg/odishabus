Core Capabilities

Everything You Need to Catch Your Odisha Bus on Time

Designed for daily commuters, students, and inter-district travelers looking for accurate Odisha bus timings and real-time locations.

01. Live GPS Positions, Directional Arrows & Realistic Coach View

Every active vehicle appears on a smooth custom-colored MapLibre vector map with clear directional heading arrows. Zoom in or tap Follow this bus to watch realistic top-down fleet coaches move along the road with live speed and AM/PM next-station arrival times.

02. Tap Any Road for Passing Routes

Click directly on any highway or town road on the map to open Routes on this road and inspect every Odisha bus service passing along that stretch.

03. Complete Odisha Station & Stop Search

Search thousands of bus stands and village stops — including Bhubaneswar BSABT, Kataka Badambadi, Puri, Sambalpur, Dhenkanal, Kamakhyanagar, Kusumajodi, and Mahuli.

04. Real GPS My Location, Viewport-Synced Explorer & Resting Indicators

Tap Center on my location to jump to your real GPS coordinates with a pulsing blue dot. Keep In map view enabled to filter the left bus list to vehicles visible inside your zoomed area, while parked or destination-arrived buses show a gentle floating Zzz sleep animation.

OSRTC Express

Mukhyamantri Bus Seva

Affordable Gram Panchayat and block connectivity fleet (formerly LAccMI) connecting villages, colleges, and district hospitals with live GPS tracking.

Ama Bus

High-frequency CRUT urban city bus service across Bhubaneswar, Kataka, Puri, Rourkela, Sambalpur, and Berhampur.

Ama Bus AC

Air-conditioned city and electric coaches serving Bhubaneswar Airport, Master Canteen, KIIT, Nandankanan, Kataka, and Puri corridors.

Mo Bus

Capital region urban commuter routes linking Bhubaneswar, Kataka, Khordha, Pipili, and Konark with transparent stop lists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Odisha Bus Live Tracking

Is Odisha Bus live tracking completely free to use?

Yes. Odisha Bus is 100% free with no login or subscription required. Open the live interactive map directly at odishabus.netlify.app on any phone or desktop browser.

Which Odisha bus fleets are supported?

Odisha Bus covers Shree Jagannath Express, OSRTC Express, Mukhyamantri Bus Seva, Ama Bus, Ama Bus AC, and Mo Bus across urban and intercity Odisha corridors.

How can I check which bus routes pass along my road?

Tap directly on any road or highway on the live Odisha Bus map. The Routes on this road panel lists every passing bus service and lets you preview its complete stop-by-stop path.

Can I see live buses near my current GPS location?

Yes. Tap Center on my location to jump to your real GPS coordinates with a blue dot, and keep In map view checked in the sidebar to see live buses moving inside your map area.
